How scoring works

Weapons and armor

Weapons 60% Aegis tier (S 100 · A 85 · B 70 · C 50 · D 30, up to +5 for rank) and 40% roll: a perk column counts when any selectable perk is one Aegis recommends. Off-sheet weapons fall back to the voltron wishlist.

Armor 40% stats vs a Tier 5 roll · 25% fit to your build stats · 25% set bonus (Aegis S–F) · 10% Tier 5 or exotic.

Encounter loadouts

One weapon per slot, at most one exotic, ranked by vault score and fit for each role. Guide picks get extra weight (Shacknews, Destructoid, Grindout, Deltia's Gaming, Game Rant).

R&Ds farm score

Raids and dungeons are ranked separately. 40% loot quality (the three best weapons on Aegis's sheet) · 40% need (S–B loot you lack or own with a weak roll) · 20% armor set you haven't completed.
